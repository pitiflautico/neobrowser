# Newsletter submission draft — NeoBrowser (2026-08-20)

## Ben's Bites / TLDR AI / AI Breakfast style

**One-liner:** NeoBrowser is an MCP server that lets AI agents drive your real Google Chrome — with your real logged-in sessions, genuine fingerprint, and human-like input.

**Why it matters:** Most AI browser tools launch a sterile headless browser that trips every login wall and bot check. NeoBrowser drives the user's actual Chrome via CDP, so agents inherit existing sessions and trust. It reports `succeeded`/`blocked`/`uncertain` based on before/after observations, not just "I clicked."
