# Newsletter pitch: real browser vs headless for AI agents

## One-liner

"Most AI browser tools launch a clean headless browser and hope the web treats it like a human. That works until the task needs a login, a cookie, or a real fingerprint."

## Pitch (50 words)

I wrote a short technical piece on why AI agents often fail not because of the model, but because the browser environment lies to them. It compares headless browsers against driving real Chrome over CDP, with honest trade-offs and a bot-detection study against live sites.
