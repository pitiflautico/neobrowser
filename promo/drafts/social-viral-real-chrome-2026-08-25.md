The hottest take in AI browser agents:

Spoofing a headless browser to look human is a losing arms race.

Every time you fake WebGL, patch `navigator.webdriver`, or randomize a fingerprint, you're betting the site won't check the *next* signal. And they always do.

We went the other way with NeoBrowser.

Instead of faking a browser, we drive the one you already use.
Real Chrome. Real cookies. Real TLS fingerprint. Real mouse events.
The agent inherits your actual trust state, so logged-in dashboards just... work.

Trade-off? It's not the fastest tool on the bench.
Playwright MCP wins on raw speed.
But there are flows it simply can't do, and walls it hits just as hard.

The honest benchmark is public: https://github.com/pitiflautico/neobrowser/blob/main/bench/study.md

If you're building agents that need to touch the real web — not just public pages — the browser environment matters more than the model prompt.
